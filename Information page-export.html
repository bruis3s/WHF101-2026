H A F WERKS is an Explorative Architecture, Objects and Spatial Design Practice, established in 2020. H A F WERKS's believe in the freedom of playing around with different styles, tweaking geometrical forms, narrating fun spatial sequences, and trying out non-building materials. With their playful approach in design, H A F WERKS built many Architectures, Interior & Product Design.
